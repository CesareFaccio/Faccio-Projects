Analyse Your Own Climb

Everything below runs on your own device. The first video takes a minute or two — the pose model (about 43 MB) downloads once, then every frame is processed locally.

Works with H.264-encoded video (MP4/MOV). If this was recorded on an iPhone, check Settings > Camera > Formats is set to “Most Compatible”, not “High Efficiency” — HEVC video can’t be played by most browsers.
