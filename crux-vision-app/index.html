Crux Vision

Pose tracking, hold detection and biomechanical force analysis for climbing video — computed entirely in your browser. Nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.

Example analysis

Left: the share of body weight carried by each hand and foot, with limb segments coloured by axial force and gauges for the centre of mass’s velocity and acceleration. Right: the climb itself, overlaid with the tracked skeleton, the holds it detected, and the centre of mass.

Analyse your own climb

Everything below runs on your own device. The first video takes a minute or two — the pose model (about 43 MB) downloads once, then every frame is processed locally.

Works with H.264-encoded video (MP4/MOV). If this was recorded on an iPhone, check Settings > Camera > Formats is set to “Most Compatible”, not “High Efficiency” — HEVC video can’t be played by most browsers.
